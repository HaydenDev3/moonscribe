# MoonScribe legal review handoff

The public policy pages are product-grounded drafts, not legal advice or counsel-approved terms.

Before launch, qualified independent counsel should review:

- Privacy and data practices: local storage, sync, collaboration, authentication providers, sessions, exports, support, and deletion.
- Terms of Service: account responsibilities, intellectual-property ownership, acceptable use, disclaimers, and limitation of liability.
- Cookies policy and the actual production cookie/storage inventory.
- Acceptable Use policy and enforcement process.
- Community and contact language, age requirements, jurisdiction, governing law, and support commitments.
- Consent records, policy versioning, retention, and deletion workflows for the launch jurisdictions.

Counsel should return written approval or required revisions. Do not mark this review complete from a page visit or internal engineering review.
